VISA Information

{{ conf.venue.city }}, {{ conf.venue.country }} is in the Schengen Area. Many nationalities require a Schengen short-stay visa.

⚠️ Apply Early

VISA processing times vary by country. We strongly recommend applying at least 6–8 weeks before travel. Conference organizers can provide an official invitation letter.

Do I Need a VISA?

{{ conf.venue.country }} is a member of the Schengen Area. EU/EEA/Swiss citizens do not need a VISA. Many other nationalities (US, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea…) can enter for short stays without a VISA. Citizens of many other countries require a Schengen Short-Stay (C) VISA. Check your country's requirements at the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs VISA portal.
